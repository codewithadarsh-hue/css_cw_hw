boAt is an Indian consumer electronics brand established in 2016 by Aman Gupta and Sameer Mehta. Operating under its parent company, Imagine Marketing Services, the brand began by selling durable, braided charging cables and quickly evolved into a massive lifestyle brand. Today, boAt is recognized as India’s No. 1 audio brand and ranks among the top wearable brands globally. Born in 2016 as Imagine Marketing Services in New Delhi, boAt set out to marry fashion with function in affordable lifestyle electronics for aspirational youth. By 2021 it cracked the global wearables top five by shipments, challenging giants like Apple and Samsung. Today boAt commands roughly 25-30% of India's earwear market and has expanded into smartwatches, audio gear, and grooming products - a rapid scale-up powered by digital-first retail and trend-driven launches. The boAt story began in November 2016 when Aman Gupta, an ex-sales director at HARMAN (JBL), and Sameer Mehta, a manufacturing entrepreneur, teamed up to solve a common Indian pain point: fragile smartphone charging cables. Their maiden product-a rugged, tangle-free, Apple-certified lightning cable-served as a focused MVP launched on Amazon, leveraging the founders' expertise in retail distribution and brand positioning. The name "boAt" and the tagline "Plug into Nirvana" framed the brand as a lifestyle play rather than a mere accessories maker. Bootstrapped with about 30 lakh INR, the founders adopted a fast-fashion electronics model aimed at youth who treated headphones as fashion statements. They prioritized e-commerce (Amazon, Flipkart) to avoid brick-and-mortar overhead, sourced higher-quality components, and tuned products for heavy bass favored by Indian consumers. Early traction came from compelling value, bold design, and digital-first marketing, laying the groundwork for rapid category expansion into audio, wearables, and mobile accessories.
